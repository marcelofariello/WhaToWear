WhaToWear is configured as an iOS Progressive Web App (PWA) with a custom Home Screen icon, full-screen standalone display, and offline wardrobe storage.

📱 Install WhaToWear on Your iPhone (Full-Screen App, Not Inside Safari)

WhaToWear includes an iOS Standalone App Manifest (display: standalone) and a custom Home Screen icon. Once added, it launches from your iPhone Home Screen like a native app—without Safari's address bar or bottom toolbar.

Option A (Easiest · 30 Seconds) · Install as a Standalone Home Screen App 1. Drag the whatowear folder onto app.netlify.com/drop (free, no coding) to get a private https:// link.
2. Open that link once on your iPhone, tap the Share button (⬆️ at the bottom), and tap ➕ Add to Home Screen.
3. From then on, tap the WhaToWear icon on your iPhone Home Screen — it opens in full-screen standalone mode (not inside Safari tabs) and stores your wardrobe on your phone.
Option B (100% Offline File on iPhone · No Hosting Needed) 1. Send index.html to your iPhone via AirDrop or save it in iCloud Drive (Files app).
2. Install the free Scriptable or Koder /Documents app from the App Store, or create an iOS Shortcut with "Get File → Show Web View" and tap Add to Home Screen. It will open directly as a dedicated full-screen app offline!
Option C (True Native iOS `.ipa` App via Capacitor) Because WhaToWear is a zero-dependency HTML5 app, you can also wrap it into a native Xcode iOS app in 3 commands (npx @capacitor/cli init → npx cap add ios → npx cap open ios) and install it onto your iPhone via USB cable.
